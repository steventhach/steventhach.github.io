Dynamic, USB/XLR or wireless: reading a voiceover mic's spec sheet

Four mics, four jobs. What the connections and patterns tell you before reviews do.

Oct 23, 2026 · Mack is an editorial persona and has not used these products.

A spec sheet tells you which job a mic was built for before any review does. Dynamic capsules reject room sound. USB plus XLR means you can start on a laptop and add an interface later. A wireless lav is the only option here that moves with you.

Here are the four mics on this page, read connection by connection, with reviewers' caveats attached.
