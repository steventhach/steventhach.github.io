No.01 · Magnetic building tiles

MAGNA-TILES Classic 32: the warning worth reading twice

Ages 3+ per the manufacturer

From the listing's warning panel

WARNING: CHOKING HAZARD -- Small parts. Not for children under 3 yrs.

WARNING: This product contains small magnets. Swallowed magnets can stick together across intestines causing serious infections and death. Seek immediate medical attention if magnets are swallowed or inhaled.

Who it's for
  • Three-to-eight-year-olds who build without instructions
  • Families who'll add sets later; every MAGNA-TILES set is compatible
Who should skip it
  • Any home with an under-3 who grabs a sibling's toys
  • Anyone expecting a big build from 32 pieces

On the listing

  • Ages 3+ per the manufacturer
  • WARNING: CHOKING HAZARD -- Small parts. Not for children under 3 yrs.
  • Contains small magnets; the listing warns swallowed magnets can cause serious injury and to seek immediate medical attention
  • Riveted tiles with the magnets sealed inside

What independent reviewers report (paraphrased from ScienceBasedKids)

  • ScienceBasedKids, reviewing the bigger 100-piece set, praises the build quality and durability for ages 3 to 8
  • The same review notes the premium over cheaper brands and that older kids may outgrow it

Theo's verdictA staple for 3+. The magnet warning is the most serious line in this guide, so keep cracked tiles out of play and littler hands away.
