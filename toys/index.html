No.01 Sesame Street by Just Play · Interactive plush In the window

Tickle Me Elmo Giggle Max

The 1996 legend, back at 18 inches, and on all three lists.

Ages 18 months+ per the manufacturer

  • Target Top 50: listed
  • Amazon Toys We Love: listed
  • Good Housekeeping: listed

From the listing

Listing safety information: May contain small pieces.

An 18-inch interactive plush with three tickle spots: the belly and both feet. Per the listing, the giggles keep building the more you tickle, while the arms move, the mouth opens and closes and the whole body shakes. It's billed as a 30th-anniversary release.

For

Toddlers and preschoolers who love loud, silly, reactive toys, and the grown-ups who remember 1996

Skip if

Anyone after a soft bedtime cuddle: reviewers describe a firm body

Peek inside: listing facts and what reviewers flag
  • 18-inch interactive plush with three tickle spots
  • Arms, mouth and body move as it giggles
  • Short phrases between giggles
  • Marked as a 30th-anniversary edition

What independent reviewers report: The Toy Insider highlights the escalating giggles, the arm-flapping and the surprise hiccups, and notes it arrives ready to play. Trendsicle's buying guide says it's widely in stock and flags the one mismatch: it's an animatronic with a firm body, not a squishy plush, and the laughing is loud and sustained for noise-wary toddlers. Sources: The Toy Insider, Trendsicle. Paraphrased; accessed Oct 23, 2026.

Nell: This one performs. Switch it out of try-me mode before it goes under the tree, or the show starts inside the box.

The listing says the included USB-C cable is 8.6 inches long to meet child-safety standards for ages 1+.

Set a figure on top and it plays stories and songs; squeeze an ear for volume, tap a side to skip. The listing adds a sleep timer, a sunrise alarm, a light ring and new Tonieplay games (controller sold separately).
