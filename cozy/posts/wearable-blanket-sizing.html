One size or three: sizing a wearable blanket you can't try on for them

Oct 23, 2026 · Wren is an editorial persona and has not used these products.

You can't ask someone to try on their gift. A wearable blanket either fits by being huge, comes in sizes, or skips the question by being a blanket with sleeves.

The three wearables on this page take one approach each. Here is what each listing actually promises on fit, and what reviewers noticed.
