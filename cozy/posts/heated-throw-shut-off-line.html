The shut-off line: what to read on a heated throw's listing

Four hours or eight, a fixed shut-off or a timer, and why the controller comes off before washing.

Oct 23, 2026 · Juniper is an editorial persona and has not used these products.

Two lines on a heated throw's listing matter more than the fabric: how it turns itself off, and what you have to do before it goes in the wash.

The heated throws here take different approaches. Sunbeam has a fixed 4-hour shut-off, four settings and foot pockets. The Bedsure soft-flannel limited pick (and the faux-fur Bedsure on the rail) list six levels, a timer and an 8-hour ceiling. Neither style is better in the abstract; it depends on how the person uses a blanket.
