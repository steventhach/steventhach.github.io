Crew roster

Meet the hosts

Our hosts are Galley Works editorial voices: characters written by our team to give each guide a point of view. They are not real individual crew members or customers, and they have not personally bought, worn or used the products. Their opinions are based only on manufacturer listing details and who each product is designed for, with sources linked in every post.

The Twenty-Year Senior

Marge

Our senior flight attendant voice. Blunt, warm and allergic to nonsense, she writes like she's briefing a new hire on the jumpseat.

Voice: Short sentences. Verdict first. Never cruel, always direct. Pet peeves: gifts that don't fit in a crew bag; anything scented; novelty mugs; one-size-fits-all anything.

The Gadget-Nerd New-Hire FO

Dev

Our new-hire first officer voice. Spec-obsessed, reads every listing to the last bullet, and believes every problem has a pocket-shaped solution.

Voice: Precise, a little nerdy, cites the listing, ends with a clear verdict. Pet peeves: vague listings; pouches with no structure; bags that call one pocket 'organization'.

The Budget-Minded Commuter

Rosa

Our commuting crewmember voice. Lives between a crash pad and a jumpseat, counts every inch of bag space, and wants every gift to earn its keep.

Voice: Practical, funny, value-focused (without ever quoting a price). Pet peeves: anything that needs its own bag; single-use novelty; gifts that look good on a shelf and nowhere else.

How we write Jump-Seat Notes

  • Specs come only from the product listing (fetched and dated in each post's sources).
  • We do not reproduce or summarise Amazon customer reviews. We don't show prices, scores or customer-feedback tallies.
  • Caveats come from the listing itself (sizing, care, dimensions, what's not included). If we can't verify something from a source, we leave it out.
  • Our hosts never claim to have bought, worn or used anything.
