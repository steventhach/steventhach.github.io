Stop Buying Crew Cute Socks. Buy Them These.

Marge's no-nonsense guide to compression socks and packing cubes: who they're for, who should skip them, and what the listings say to check first.

By Marge, a Galley Works editorial voice. Marge is a character written by our team, not a real crewmember or customer, and has not personally bought, worn or used these products. About our hosts.

Here's the verdict before the briefing, honey: the best crew gifts look boring on paper and are built for hour twelve. Below are three picks from our guide, with what the listings say and what to check before you buy. I haven't worn or packed any of them; I'm a Galley Works editorial voice, and I'm reading the same listings you can.

Item 01

Graduated compression socks · GW 001

Sockwell Circulator: the one to give if you're giving one

Official brand video (YouTube) — not filmed by us. Watch on YouTube

Cleared Who it's for

  • Flight attendants who are on their feet through long days and want compression that doesn't look clinical
  • Anyone who would rather have a merino-blend sock than a purely synthetic one

Leave it Who should skip it

  • Shoppers who don't know the recipient's shoe size (it comes in two size bands)
  • Anyone who needs firmer compression; this one is labelled moderate

What the listing says

  • Labelled moderate (15-20 mmHg) graduated compression in four zones
  • Merino wool, rayon, nylon and spandex blend; hand wash, line dry
  • Full arch support, reinforced toe and heel, seamless toe closure
  • Two sizes by women's US shoe size (4-7.5 and 8-11); sits over the calf

Check before you buy

  • Hand wash and line dry, per the care label, so it suits someone who doesn't mind a sink wash on a layover
  • Sizes are by women's US shoe size only (4-7.5 and 8-11); larger feet are outside the listed range
  • Moderate compression is the stated level; nothing on the listing suggests a firmer option for this model

Marge's verdictGive it to the person who stands for a living. Get the size right: on paper, it's the pick on this page built most directly for long days on your feet.

Item 02

Unisex compression socks · GW 008

Physix Gear: the safe bet, with a sizing caveat

Official brand video (YouTube) — not filmed by us. Watch on YouTube

Cleared Who it's for

  • Pilots, new hires and anyone whose style you don't know: it's unisex and comes in plenty of colours
  • People who want a firmer feel; the title lists 20-30 mmHg

Leave it Who should skip it

  • Anyone whose calf measurement falls outside the listed size chart
  • Someone who wants a natural-fibre sock; the listing says nylon and spandex

What the listing says

  • Title lists 20-30 mmHg compression, for men and women
  • Nylon and spandex; machine wash cold, tumble dry low per the care label
  • Size chart by shoe size and calf circumference, from S/M up to XXL

Check before you buy

  • Measure the calf: the size chart lists calf circumference ranges alongside shoe sizes
  • Machine wash cold and tumble dry low, per the care label
  • Nylon-spandex construction, so skip it for someone who only wears natural fibres

Marge's verdictMeasure the calf, read the chart, then buy. Skip the guesswork and it's a dependable gift.

Item 03

Regular packing cubes (6-set) · GW 004

BAGAIL packing cubes: for the four-day trip that won't zip

Official brand video (YouTube) — not filmed by us. Watch on YouTube

Cleared Who it's for

  • Crew who pack and unpack every few days and want uniform, layover clothes and odds and ends kept apart
  • People who like to see what's inside; the cubes have mesh top panels

Leave it Who should skip it

  • Minimalists who travel with one outfit and a toothbrush
  • Anyone who wants compression cubes; the listing doesn't describe these as compression cubes

What the listing says

  • A six-piece set in mixed sizes, made of 290D fabric per the listing
  • Mesh top panels for seeing contents and ventilation
  • Hand wash recommended; gentle machine wash and air dry allowed

Check before you buy

  • Gentle machine wash and air dry are allowed; the listing warns against a hot dryer
  • It's a six-piece set in mixed sizes, so check it suits the recipient's bag size
  • No compression zips are listed, so it organises rather than shrinks a load

Marge's verdictA crew bag should zip shut on day four. For most crew, cubes are how.
