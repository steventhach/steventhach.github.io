Draft-night rules for families

Rule 1: First-round picks are sacred

Each person gets one Round 1 wish. If that's the only gift they get from you, they're happy. Everything else is bonus.

Rule 2: The commissioner holds the cap

One person (you, probably, since you're reading this) owns the total and the per-person caps. Not sure what those should be? Our guide on how much to spend on Christmas gifts has a free splitter that turns your cash into a cap for every person.

Rule 3: Backups protect the budget

Round 1 is out of stock or over the cap? Go to Round 2, not to a bigger number.

Rule 4: Draft early, ship on time

USPS recommends sending by Thu Dec 17 (Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail), Fri Dec 18 (Priority Mail) or Sat Dec 19 (Priority Mail Express) for expected delivery before Dec 25 in the lower 48, per its 2026 holiday shipping page. Lists finished in November give you every option.

Rule 5: Claim it, then lock it

When someone commits to buying a pick, mark it on the board. Double-gifting is a draft-day blunder.

Christmas list template for kids vs. adults

Kids: big boxes, room to draw, and an age on every line (yes, even if you think everyone knows). Teens: let them type it; insist on sizes. Adults: links are fine, but add "or similar" so the shopper can stay under the cap without a hunt for one exact listing. Grandparents: NRF's October 2026 survey found 59% of Boomers want gift cards. If that's their Round 1, write the store name and the cap, and you're done.

FAQ

How do I make a Christmas list template?

Give every person a row with a budget cap, a first-choice wish, a second choice, a backup and a 'do not buy' note. Add specifics like size, age range and color. The free Family Wish-List Draft Board on this page does that and can be printed or copied as text.

Is there a free printable Christmas list template?

Yes. This page has a free, letter-size printable PDF of the Family Wish-List Draft Board with eight rows, a cap box per person and a total line. No sign-up needed.

What should be on a Christmas list?

A name, a budget cap, wishes in order of importance, and specifics a shopper can act on: size, age range, color, model or 'or similar.' A short 'do not buy' note prevents duplicates and returns.

How many items should be on a Christmas list?

Three per person works well for a family list: a first pick, a second pick and a backup. That gives the shopper options without turning the list into a catalog.

How do I make a Christmas list for the whole family?

Use one board with a row per person, set one total budget, and share it as a photo or a copied text list in the family chat. If you want the gifts matched to a budget automatically, paste the list into CartMi.

Can I make a Christmas list in Google Docs?

Yes. Fill in the Draft Board on this page, tap 'Copy as a clean list,' and paste the text into a Google Doc, a note or a message.
