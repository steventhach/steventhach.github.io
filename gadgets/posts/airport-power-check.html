The airport power check: adapter, power bank, data blocker

Three small fixes for three travel-day power problems, and the fine print on each listing.

Oct 23, 2026 · Mara is an editorial persona and has not used these products.

Travel-day power problems come in three shapes: the wrong outlet, a phone that dies before the gate, and a public USB port you'd rather not trust. The MagSafe MagGo pack on the limited section is a fourth path when the phone snaps to a pack instead of a cable.

Each of the three gadgets here handles one of them. Read the fine print first; each listing or maker page has a line that matters.
