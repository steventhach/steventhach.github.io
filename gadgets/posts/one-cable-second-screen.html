One cable, second screen: reading a portable monitor's ports before you pack it

What USB-C, mini-HDMI and 'no battery' mean for a 15.6-inch screen in a laptop bag, plus the light bar that frees the desk it sits on.

Oct 23, 2026 · Ines is an editorial persona and has not used these products.

A portable monitor either works with your laptop over a single cable or turns into a drawer ornament. Which one you get depends on the port on your laptop, not the monitor.

Here's the 15.6-inch screen on this page read port by port, with the reviewers' caveats. After it comes the light bar that frees up the desk the screen sits on.
