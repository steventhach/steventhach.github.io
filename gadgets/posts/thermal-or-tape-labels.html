Thermal 4x6 or TZe tape: two label makers for two different jobs

Shipping labels and cable labels need different machines. Here's what each listing says it does, and the catches.

Oct 23, 2026 · Theo is an editorial persona and has not used these products.

A shipping label and a cable label are different jobs. One is a 4x6 sheet printed from a marketplace PDF; the other is a strip of label tape you type on the spot.

The two machines on this page split along that line. Here is what each listing says it does, and where each one stops.
