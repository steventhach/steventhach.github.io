§4 · Exploded view

Tap a part. Read what the listing won't tell you.

SELECT A PART

Six parts, six questions.

Every number on a vacuum listing belongs to one of these parts. Tap one to see what to check.

PART 01 · BATTERY

The runtime lives here

Find the mode and attachment behind the headline minutes. "At the handheld" means no powered floor head.

PART 02 · MOTOR

Power, in watts or air watts

W is input power; AW is suction. Listings mix the two, so compare like with like.

PART 03 · FILTER

Filter vs sealed system

"HEPA filter" and "sealed system" are different claims. The seal stops dust leaking out around the filter.

PART 04 · DUST BIN

The bin nobody measures

Reviewers flag small bins first. With a shedder, a dock or a bigger bin beats more suction.

PART 05 · WAND

Bend, length, storage

A bending wand reaches under the sofa; a self-standing design saves you drilling a wall mount.

PART 06 · FLOOR HEAD

Where hair wraps

Look for a named anti-wrap design: fins, combs, a self-cleaning roller. "Pet" in the name isn't one.
