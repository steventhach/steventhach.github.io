Where the numbers come from

  • Specs: Amazon product listings (bullets and spec tables) and manufacturer spec pages, retrieved Oct 23, 2026. Listings change; check before you buy.
  • Reviewer themes: paraphrased from independent publications, linked on every item. We never quote or summarise Amazon customer reviews.
  • Ranking: verifiable demand (Amazon "bought in past month" badges on Oct 23, 2026), how completely each listing states its specs and conditions, and how consistent reviewer themes are. We haven't used these vacuums.
  • No prices or scores: prices change hourly, so we link to the listing instead.
  • Pictures: original line drawings of a generic stick vacuum. Product photos appear only where Amazon serves them through its approved tools.
