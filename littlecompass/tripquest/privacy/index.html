The short version

TripQuest is a vacation photo-quest game for children that works completely offline. We do not collect, store on our servers, share, sell or analyze any information about you or your child. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app creates stays on your phone.

What the app does on the phone

  • Photos and short video clips. When your child takes a picture or a "Moving Moment" clip for a mission, it is saved only in TripQuest's private storage on the device. Other apps and the phone's photo gallery cannot see it. The clips have no sound: TripQuest never asks for the microphone.
  • Game progress. Quest progress, trading cards, settings (music, sounds, the kid's mute button) and, if a grown-up types one in, the child's first name (used only so Gallie can say it on screen) are saved in the same private storage.
  • Gallie's voice. Gallie's lines are recordings built into the app. If a recording is missing, the phone's own text-to-speech engine may read the line aloud. TripQuest sends nothing to it beyond the words on screen.

None of this ever leaves the device unless a grown-up chooses to share it (see below).

No internet, no data collection

  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.
  • We use no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking tools of any kind.
  • There are no accounts, logins, chat or social features, and no way for children to contact anyone through the app.
  • TripQuest does not collect location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising IDs or any other personal information.

Permissions

The camera is the only permission TripQuest asks for. It is used only while your child is taking a mission photo or clip. You can turn it off at any time in the phone's Settings; the rest of the game still works.

Purchases

The Starter Quest is free. A grown-up can buy the optional, one-time Full Adventure unlock (US $4.99; the store shows the price in your currency). There is no subscription.

  • Buying and restoring happen only in the Grown-ups' Corner, behind a parent gate.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. TripQuest never sees your card or payment details. The app only learns whether the Full Adventure was bought, and remembers that on the phone. Google's own privacy policy covers the purchase: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Sharing (grown-ups only)

A movie or photo leaves the phone only when a grown-up passes the parent gate and taps Share with Family or Save to Photos. That opens the phone's own share sheet, and you choose the app and the person (for example Messages, Gmail or Save Image). TripQuest does not send anything itself and never sees who you share with. Share copies are deleted from the app when the share panel closes and every time the app starts.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

TripQuest is made for children and their families. Because the app collects no personal information from children, and nothing is transmitted off the device, no parental consent for data collection is needed. All purchases and sharing sit behind a parent gate. We follow the Google Play Families Policy.

Backups, keeping and deleting data

  • TripQuest turns off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so its data is not copied to Google Drive or a new phone.
  • To delete everything, uninstall the app (or open the phone's Settings > Apps > TripQuest > Storage > Clear storage). This removes all photos, clips, progress and settings permanently. We have no copy, so we cannot restore it.
  • Photos a grown-up shared or saved elsewhere stay wherever you put them.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships. We will never begin collecting children's personal information without first getting verifiable parental consent.
